## 1. What this is

A design-assignment POC for an interview. **Brief (from the interviewer, Vithya, via Priya):** design an e-commerce website for an auto-care brand selling **spare parts, tyres and tracks** for multiple vehicle brands and categories (cars, trucks, tractors, other vehicles). Only three screens wanted: **Home, one Product Listing Page (PLP), one Product Detail Page (PDP)**. The PDP must let a user check **compatibility/fitment** and find **local dealers** to buy from.

What they care about most is the **thinking**, not just screens: how I understood the industry, identified users (personas), journey mapping, information architecture, how I'd use AI / natural-language search, and how friendly and seamless the experience is. Format reference: the same author's earlier case-study docs (YourDOST, Mumzworld) — **used for document structure only, never their colours or UI**.

Deliverables (both open straight from the folder, no build needed):

| File | What it is |
|---|---|

| `prototype.html` | The working prototype (Home, Listing, Product). Single file, generated by `src/build.py`. Needs `assets/` next to it. |

| `src/` | Editable source: `style.css`, `shell.html`, `data.js`, `app.js`, `build.py`. |

Brand name **"Fitwise"** is a placeholder. All prices, part numbers, dealers and statistics inside the prototype are illustrative.

## 2. How to run and rebuild

```bash
cd "<this folder>"
python3 -m http.server 5173        # then open http://localhost:5173/index.html
python3 src/build.py               # regenerates prototype.html from src/
```

- `build.py` inlines `style.css`, the `shell.html` template (an HTML template literal; uses `${I('icon')}`, `${PH('img')}`, `${C('img')}` helpers) and `data.js` + `app.js` into `prototype.html`. It loads Lucide icons from unpkg and Inter / JetBrains Mono / Noto from Google Fonts.
- `index.html` is hand-written HTML (not generated). Its prototype embeds are lazy iframes pointing at `prototype.html?embed=1#home|plp|pdp`.
- Deep links useful for testing: `prototype.html#home`, `#plp`, `#pdp`, `?ask=<question>` (opens the assistant with that question), `?embed=1` (hides dock/util bar).
- **macOS gotcha:** preview servers launched by tools may be blocked from reading `~/Downloads` (TCC). If a server can't start, serve a copy from another folder.

## 3. How the user wants it to look (their feedback, in order, distilled)

The user (Chaitanya, the candidate) iterated hard on UI. Their words were blunt; the lesson is the bar is **"top-tier Indian/global e-commerce lead designer shipped this"**, not "AI made this".

1. First pass was rejected as cluttered/AI-looking: drawn SVG product art, too many pills and gradients, big dark AI chat block, reviewer "design notes" pins and dock. They wanted real images "assuming it is the real product", Indian vehicles, and a cleaner UI.
2. **AI placement:** they asked for a floating AI like Jio Copilot on Fynd stores, then said *don't make it floating just because I said it; research how commerce products do it and apply best UX*. Decision: AI lives in search, a contextual "Help me choose" on the listing, "Ask about this part" on the PDP, and "Customers say" review summaries. All open **one docked side panel** (bottom sheet on mobile). No floating bubble, no big home-page chat.
3. **Hero:** carousel on the left was "distracting"; the vehicle finder on the right is the important part. They wanted a centred, bigger finder, **a CRED-style number-plate input in light mode**, and **brand logos below it** (Spinny/Cars24 pattern). Later: hero looked empty, then "too much blue", then "not real product vibe / feels vibe-coded". Final: calm studio hero (finder left, official Creta render right, three proof points).
4. **Garage:** they noticed "saved vehicles" appearing without sign-in. Fix: the header shows a **signed-in profile** (Arjun). The garage became a pill switcher plus one large vehicle card, not a row of small cards.
5. **Colour:** "Is the primary black or blue? confusing." Then "too much blue, don't look AI-ish". Final system is in section 4.
6. **Categories vs products:** "there is no difference between products and categories now". Fix: categories are large angled part renders on warm gradient tiles; products sit on plain white wells with a hairline border.
7. **3D icons:** I tried Fluent 3D emoji; rejected as **childish**. Don't use emoji-style 3D icons.
8. **Brand logos:** must be real colour logos (not mono). Use the manufacturer's own studio image for a saved vehicle, e.g. Hyundai's Creta render.
9. **Toasts:** position/styling was "worst". Now light cards top-right with a product thumbnail and a "View cart" link (bottom, full-width on mobile).
10. References they named: CRED, Cars24, Spinny, Baymard e-commerce principles, Behance/Dribbble/real car sites for icon inspiration, Jio Copilot on Fynd (only as a pattern to evaluate).

### Rejected (do not reintroduce)
- Auto-scrolling hero carousel; dusk/stock photo hero with glass chips.
- Cobalt/blue as the dominant colour; gradient headline words; sparkle icons.
- Drawn/illustrated product art; Fluent 3D emoji icons.
- Floating chat bubble; large AI chat card on Home.
- Reviewer dock and numbered "design note" pins in the prototype.
- Photo banners of street scenes (MRF shop, JCB on a street) as offer cards.
- Black "Find my vehicle" next to blue CTAs (inconsistent primary).

## 4. Design system (current)

**Colour tokens** (`:root` in `src/style.css`):
- Ink `#16181B` text **and every primary action** (`.btn.sun` is a legacy class name that is now ink-black).
- Stone `#F6F5F1` / tile `#F3F2EE` warm surfaces. Lines `#E8E6E1`.
- Copper accent `#C0703A` / `--offer #B8612E`: offers and "due soon" only.
- Fit green `#2F7A52`: fits, ratings, savings. Amber `#9A6A1E`: "check variant". Red `#C8343A`: doesn't fit.
- Plate blue `#2342C8`: **only** the IND strip on number plates.
- Secondary buttons: white with ink outline. Links: underlined ink text. Cite Baymard: buttons and links must not be confused; colour carries meaning, not decoration.

**Type:** Inter (headlines 800, tight tracking; tabular numbers for prices), JetBrains Mono for part numbers/sizes, Noto Sans Devanagari/Tamil fallbacks. **Icons:** Lucide. **Radii:** 10 to 20 px; **motion:** springs for confirmation, shimmer only for "working", no animation on prices/stock; reduced-motion respected.

**Patterns used** (inspired by the component libraries the user sent: UI Arc, Kobra, Spectrum UI, Space UI, Bencho, Lightswind, Beautiful UI, etc.; rebuilt in plain HTML/CSS/JS): CRED-style plate input, command-palette search with parsed-intent chips, reasoning steps + streaming answer in the assistant, segmented control, range histogram slider, filter chips, radio cards, slide-to-confirm reserve, hotspot "where it goes" gallery, compare tray + drawer, toast with undo.

## 5. Product decisions worth knowing

- **Hero journey:** "my steering shakes when I brake" -> warped front brake discs for a **2024 Hyundai Creta SX 1.5 CRDi** (OE part `51712-C9000`). Chosen because free studio photos of rotors exist (car brake pads barely do). Pads appear as "frequently bought together".
- **Thesis:** fitment is the product. Vehicle-first, AI-translates / catalogue-decides, locally fulfilled (reserve at a store, pay at the counter). North star metric: **first-time-right fitment rate**.
- **Personas (assumption-based):** Arjun (car owner, product manager), Ravi (garage mechanic), Gurpreet (farmer, tractor), Meena (fleet manager). Two-axis framing: who decides the part x cost of downtime.
- **Fitment UX:** PDP fit box states green/amber/red with evidence chips (variant match, OE number, "1,284 fitted on a Creta"). PLP asks only one disambiguating question when it changes the answer (16 vs 17-inch wheels; 80 vs 84 track links).
- **Three catalogues, one template:** brake discs, tyres, tracks have different facets (disc type; size/use/speed; width x pitch x links).
- **Garage vehicles** (`VEHICLES` in `data.js`): Creta (official Hyundai 360 frames, drag to rotate), Mahindra 575 DI, Tata Signa tipper, JCB 30 Plus. Fit logic is `fitOf()` in `app.js`; switching vehicle re-renders everything.

### How the cut-outs were made
Subject lifting with macOS Vision (Swift CLI, 15 lines): `VNGenerateForegroundInstanceMaskRequest` -> `generateMaskedImage(... croppedToInstancesExtent: true)` -> PNG, then `sips -s format avif -s formatOptions 70` for size (~40 KB vs ~900 KB PNG). Free photo APIs: Unsplash search pages need a browser (the JSON API needs a key; scripted scraping was blocked), Wikimedia Commons API works but rate-limits (429): throttle requests.

## 8. Gotchas already hit

- CSS class collision: hero carousel `.slide` vs slide-to-confirm `.slide` broke the hero; the control is now `.slider`.
- One AVIF (the full-size Creta hero) decoded as fully transparent in the browser while valid elsewhere; the hero car is a **PNG** on purpose.
- Image wells need an explicit grid track (`grid-template-rows:100%`) or percentage-sized images collapse.
- Home uses lazy images; when screenshotting, force `loading=eager` first.
- Many Simple Icons brand marks (Hero, Bajaj, Tata...) are mono or missing; that is why colour PNG logos from Commons are used now.
- The preview pane used while developing this doc's screenshots does not always give a truly clean reload on repeat `file://` navigations to the same path (old async timers/DOM state can bleed into a "fresh" tab, and even an in-page `location.reload()` wasn't reliable there); when a test result looks impossible, open a brand-new tab or verify the logic in an isolated object instead of trusting that environment's reload.

## 8a. The assistant's AI orb (`#cpOrb`)

The small `.orb` icon used everywhere else (search bar, "Ask about this part", etc.) is a static CSS conic-gradient — cheap, and plenty for an icon-sized marker. The one in the assistant panel's own header (`.cp-h .orb`, the `.gl` variant, 36px) is the product's single "this is the AI" avatar, so it alone gets a richer canvas-drawn treatment: `initOrb`/`paintOrb`/`startOrb`/`stopOrb` in `app.js`, config in `ORB_CFG` (glow, noise/shimmer detail, glints, halo, colour speed, hover tilt — tuned to the values a design brief gave for a commercial "AI Blob" React component). It draws a soft halo, a shimmering segmented rim, a pulsing core and a glint that leans toward the cursor, all in the app's own ink/red palette rather than that component's default four-colour rainbow — "reactivity" maps to the one real signal this text assistant has, whether it's actively thinking (`.think`, already toggled by `runAI`), not an audio level that doesn't exist here. `startOrb()` runs only while the panel is open (hooked into `openCopilot`/`closeCopilot`); `prefers-reduced-motion` draws one static frame and stops, matching the referenced component's own "still orb" behaviour.

## 10. Questions the doc raises for the interview

What happens to a registration number: which registry or provider is queried, what is kept, for how long, and what consent and notice the DPDP Act 2023 needs (the prototype copy deliberately makes no "we don't store it" promise)? Brand model (manufacturer vs marketplace)? What fitment data exists and who maintains it? Dealer-network exclusivity and who owns the customer after a counter sale? B2C vs B2B mix? Which vehicle category leads? How far should AI go (suggest vs diagnose with a partner garage)?
